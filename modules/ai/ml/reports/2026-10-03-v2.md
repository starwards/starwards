# Snapshot scorer v2, 2026-10-03

Artefact `modules/ai/src/scoring/models/v2.json`; full generated output (every table, per scenario, LOSO,
reliability bins, leakage checks) in `2026-10-03-v2-training.md`. Protocol: `../README.md`. Jev spend $0:

## Data

`training-archive/datasets/snapshots-2026-10-03-v2.csv`: every run in the archive (2026-10-02 and
2026-10-03), 2778 runs, 409 006 frames, 56 features, 8 labels. Built in about 2 h 10 min.

- Tactical and weapons labels need the `shot`/`damage` sidecar events. Runs recorded at the commits that
  write them (`weapons-score/`, `weapons-score-superseded/`) or whose sidecar has any carry them; every
  other run is censored for `tactical45`, `opportunity45` and `conversion45`, never imputed. 636 runs carry them (148 050 frames).
- `conversion45` is censored where O = 0.
- `TACTICAL_WEIGHTS.rho` is the weapons report's fitted 0.0272 (it was a 0.01 placeholder).

## Features added (one frame each)

| feature                   | why                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `t_solution`              | the frame term of O (threat-weighted firing solution now); O is its mean    |
| `t_target_threat_share`   | θ̃ of the opponent: the weight its damage earns in T                         |
| `t_threat`                | summed enemy θ before normalising: how dangerous the frame is               |
| `t_enemies`               | living enemies (credit and threat split over them)                          |
| `t_target_incapacitation` | the opponent's I: C_max's headroom is 1 − I                                 |
| `t_own_incapacitation`    | own I: what the enemy has already taken                                     |
| `w_ammo_shortfall`        | the loaded shell's shortfall against the best one: K_w's dominated term     |
| `w_tubes_ready`           | a working tube and a missile: the torpedo solution path of O                |
| `e_demand`, `e_service`   | the demand and service the frame shows (helm requests, lock, contacts)      |
| `e_risk`                  | the engineer score's risk r without the blast rate (that needs the sidecar) |
| `e_backlog`               | mean defect severity: what repairs would clear                              |

None reads the sidecar or another frame. v1 is read by feature name, so it still scores.

Rule (owner, 2026-10-03): v2 replaces v1 unless a scenario regresses beyond noise: Δ loss (v2 − v1, 95% bootstrap CI over runs) counts as a regression only when the whole CI lies above a noise floor (0.002 logloss for `kill60`, 0.0005 mse for `damage30`; the calibration floor alone costs up to 0.001 logloss) on at least 5 runs. A CI above the floor on fewer runs is reported as insufficient evidence.

The rows marked **worse** above are strict (CI above 0). Under the rule none regresses: every strict
"worse" on a near-zero-loss rung (helms-*, signals-scan, weapons-acquire, T0 damage30) lies inside the
floor. T1-noweave damage30 (+0.0028 [0.0026, 0.0030]) is above the floor on 2 runs: **insufficient
evidence**, still listed. **v2 is the default for every head.**

## What could be wrong

- The tactical heads rest on 636 runs from six scripted scenarios with scripted crews; Jev or
  human crews may sit outside that distribution (INFERENCE).
- `conversion45` is a ratio; windows with small O are noisy.
- Seed-holdout test for the tactical heads covers T1-MK2, W-multi and W-outranged only (T0/T1 test seeds
  predate the events); LOSO covers all six.
- Calibration floor 0.001 is a choice that costs ~0.001 logloss on all-zero scenarios.
